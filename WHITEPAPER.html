Part 1: Whitepaper Summary

1. Executive Summary

ChainBadge is an ultra-lightweight, gas-optimized on-chain digital credential registry deployed natively on the Botchain blockchain. Designed to recognize early adopters and active participants, ChainBadge permanently records the "Early Achiever" milestone directly onto the blockchain ledger.

2. Key Architectural Advantages

  • Strict Single-Claim Enforcement: Each wallet address can claim the achievement badge only once. Duplicate claims are permanently rejected at the EVM opcode level.
  • Zero Centralized Dependencies: Completely eliminated databases, centralized server APIs, and off-chain storage providers. All state reads and writes occur directly against the smart contract.
  • On-Chain Timestamp Attestation: Precise block timestamps are permanently bound to the claimant address, providing indisputable cryptographic proof of early adoption.
  • Composability for Botchain DAOs: External smart contracts and decentralized protocols can query hasClaimed(address) with single-slot O(1) gas cost for airdrop whitelisting and role gating.

3. Technical Specifications
